Chess Timed Trainer v2.7

Paste FENs or load a PGN/TXT file, then let the board auto-advance after each thinking interval. The solution note can appear automatically during the review phase.
How to use: load a PGN, choose a time mode, press Start, enter your move on the board, then review the solution and engine line when the timer expires. Use Review wrong positions or Review due for repetition.

Import positions

Accepted input: one FEN per line, PGN with [FEN "..."] tags, or JSON exported from this trainer.
